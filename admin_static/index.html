Make your allowance go further

  • Increase POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS from 60 to 120 to halve scheduled checks. Short flights may pass between checks.
  • Stop watches you no longer need. Promo invitations share your owner allowance; own-credential chats use their account.
  • Optional quiet-area mode: set IDLE_POLL_MAX_SECONDS=300 to slow empty watches up to five minutes. Seeing aircraft restores the base interval. Short visits can be missed.
  • Identical OpenSky searches on the same provider instance reuse results for ten seconds. Next: share regional searches between nearby watches on the same account. adsbdb metadata is already cached.

OpenSky balance is the last owner response, not a live account total. Customer balances stay separate and are not aggregated. Backoff shows the latest observed 429 window; for customer accounts it may affect only some chats.
